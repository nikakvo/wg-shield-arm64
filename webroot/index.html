Tunnels
Waiting for data…
Tap a country to see only its tunnels, tap it again to see all. Test measures each server address of the box. ★ marks a favorite (the Favorites box). The tunnel that is on stays at the top. One tunnel at a time: switching one on switches the other off. All off = your normal connection, no kill switch. Clear all deletes every imported tunnel (tap twice to confirm); the tunnel turns off.
Backup
Looking for a backup…
Backup saves every tunnel (and your stars) to /storage/emulated/0/wg-shield/wg-shield-backup.zip, the same kind of zip the WireGuard app exports. There is one backup: a new one replaces it. It stays when you remove the module, so you can bring your tunnels back after a reinstall or a Clear all. Restore imports them again: a tunnel with the same name is replaced, the others stay. The files hold your private keys, like an export in Download.
Import from Download
Looking for exports…
Exports hold your private keys. In the WireGuard app: ⋮ → Export tunnels (saved to Download). An AmneziaWG config (.conf) works the same way: put it in Download. Import it here, then delete the file. Imported tunnels are kept where only root can read them.
Protection
Kill switchA tunnel is on but not working → no internet at all, never your mobile or Wi-Fi connection directly. Off: the phone goes direct until the tunnel answers again.
LAN DNS through the tunnelOn: while the tunnel is up, name lookups sent to a resolver on your Wi-Fi (the router) go to the tunnel's DNS too, so the router does not see them. Names only the router knows (printer.lan) stop resolving. While the tunnel is connecting or down the router is asked again and sees those lookups - a login page (hotel, airport) needs that. Off (default): the router's resolver is asked directly. Nothing changes while DNSCrypt Proxy answers DNS.
Checks
Check every
Auto-switch on failureWhen the tunnel stops answering, WG Shield restarts it twice at once (always). On: if that does not help, it tries your other tunnels - the same country first (wg-BG-14 → other BG tunnels), then any other; never a server address that already failed, the fastest by the last speed test first. Off: only the restarts.
NotificationOne notification when the tunnel could not be fixed (or after a switch to another tunnel), saying what was tried - so you know you have no internet without opening the manager.
Pause
Your normal connection for a while, then the tunnel comes back by itself. A reboot ends a pause.
Maintenance
Check now: put the tunnel and its routing rules in the wanted state at once (the watchdog does it every 10 s). Report: everything needed to troubleshoot, ready to copy - private keys are never in it.
Restart: stops the watchdog, builds the tunnel again (new connection to the server) and starts the watchdog again - for when something looks stuck. Takes a few seconds; the kill switch keeps blocking meanwhile, nothing leaks. Tap twice to confirm.
